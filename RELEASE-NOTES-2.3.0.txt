JRL Messages 2.3.0 - Wake Mac, legible composer icons, scaled chrome

The missing-texts diagnosis
Texts sometimes exist on the iPhone but never reach this app, and no
Refresh, Recover, or app restart helps, yet sending any message makes
everything flood in with correct timestamps. The cause sits upstream of
Windows: Messages on the Mac goes quiet and Apple holds the incoming
texts back from the Mac entirely, so BlueBubbles has nothing to serve.
Sending cures it by accident, because an AppleScript send activates
Messages and forces it to reconnect to Apple.

Wake Mac
- A new full-width accent button below Recover performs that cure on
  purpose. It calls the BlueBubbles route POST /api/v1/mac/imessage/restart,
  which quits and reopens Messages on the Mac. Messages reconnects to
  Apple, and Apple delivers everything it was holding, in order, with
  original timestamps. Nothing is sent to anyone; nothing local changes.
- After the Mac accepts, the footer watches for about a minute, forces
  re-checks at 4, 9, 16, 30, and 50 seconds on top of the normal 3-second
  poll, shows the recovered count live, and closes with an honest total,
  including "the Mac was holding nothing back" when that is the truth.
- The button refuses, with an explanation, while one of your own messages
  is queued or on the wire, because quitting Messages mid-send could
  interrupt a delivery to a client. Failed sends waiting for your click do
  not block it.
- A server too old for the route produces a clear message naming the fix.
  Ctrl+Shift+M is the shortcut. Recover cancels any wake in progress, and
  stale wake timers or callbacks from a retired backend can never land.
- Boundary, unchanged and worth restating: Wake Mac revives an idle
  Messages app. It cannot wake a Mac that is actually asleep or offline;
  the call itself will fail visibly in that case.

Legible, scale-true composer
- The attach and emoji icons were drawn at 16 and 17 pixels inside their
  buttons; that is why they were barely visible. They are now 22 pixels
  inside 38-pixel circular targets with proper hover, pressed, and focus
  states, and every dimension follows the text-size setting, reaching
  33 pixels inside 57-pixel targets at Largest.
- The send circle grew to match, and the conversation header's refresh
  and group buttons were harmonized to the same visual weight.

Sleeker, scale-aware chrome
- Corner radii, input paddings, scrollbar tracks, menu and tooltip
  paddings now all scale with the chosen text size instead of staying
  fixed while the text grows around them.
- The pane divider highlights in the accent when hovered, scrollbar
  handles tint toward the accent on hover in both directions, and
  pressed buttons use the accent-blended surface.

Fixed: silently dropped button styling
- A doubled closing brace inside the Recover button's stylesheet made Qt
  silently discard its disabled and focus rules ever since 2.2.0; the
  console warning was invisible under pythonw. Fixed, covered by a
  regression test that scans every UI file for the pattern, and the
  offscreen harness now fails on any stylesheet parse warning at all.

Hardening
- The 15-second watchdog now also detects a dead send, download, or push
  worker, not only a dead reconciler, and performs the usual automatic
  recovery; a silently dead sender would otherwise strand queued texts.
- The wake worker participates in the same retired-backend lifecycle as
  every other worker: its HTTP client stays alive until the bounded call
  finishes, and worker references are never dropped while running.

Validation
- 43 automated tests pass: the full 2.2.0 suite plus new coverage for the
  restart route and password, 404 mapping, the outbox activity gate, the
  wake wiring contracts, composer icon sizing, and the stylesheet
  regression.
- Two offscreen GUI harnesses pass: full window construction at Default
  and Largest scales with exact pixel checks, the complete wake state
  machine, and a live end-to-end run of the real worker thread against a
  mock BlueBubbles server on both the success and 404 paths.
- All Python modules compile.

Upgrade
1. Close JRL Messages.
2. Extract this ZIP over the existing jrl-messages folder and allow
   Windows to replace files. AppData settings and the local message
   database are separate and are not removed.
3. Run install.bat once.
4. Start JRL-Messages.bat and confirm the title bar shows v2.3.0.
5. When texts you can see on the iPhone have not arrived here, press
   Wake Mac or Ctrl+Shift+M and watch the footer report what came in.
