JRL Messages 3.3.2 - Find my iPhone: identification by proof

What your screenshot showed
Two failures at once. The Windows paired list came back empty (so your
phone was not sitting at the top under its real name the way it should
have), and the remaining Apple entries were all anonymous rotating
addresses, which no human should be asked to choose between. Worse, my
paired-list failure was silent: it logged to a debug channel nobody
sees. Both of those are fixed, and the whole approach got stronger.

The headline: Find my iPhone
The picker now has a Find my iPhone button that identifies your phone
by proof instead of by name or address. Each Apple device in range is
briefly asked one question: do you serve Apple's notification service
to this PC? Only an iPhone that is paired with this PC answers yes, so
a yes is not a guess, it is your phone. The probe is a harmless
glance: it never pairs and never subscribes, so pointing it at a
neighbor's device does nothing to them. When the verified row appears
(marked "YOUR IPHONE - verified"), press OK and you are done.

Also in this release
1. The paired-device lookup now asks Windows three different ways,
   including the same association-endpoint query the Windows Settings
   page itself uses, for both the LE and the classic (voice) sides.
   Your phone's real name from the classic pairing now shows too.
2. Every discovery step reports what it found or exactly why it
   failed, in the picker itself and in the Activity panel. No more
   silently empty lists.
3. Anonymous Apple devices now carry a signal-strength hint (very
   close, nearby, in range) and sort strongest first, so the phone in
   your hand tops the anonymous group.
4. The background link uses the same proof to heal itself: when the
   phone's rotating address changes and the stored one stops working,
   the app probes the strongest Apple devices nearby, finds the one
   that serves notifications to this PC, reconnects, and remembers
   the new address automatically. You never re-pick.
5. If you ever do pick the wrong device, the link now says exactly
   that ("does not serve iPhone notifications, probably not your
   iPhone") instead of a generic connection error, and starts looking
   for the right one on its own.

Proof, per this project's standard
The radio cannot run in my build environment, so this release adds the
next best thing: the entire worker session now runs end to end in the
test suite against a scripted radio: subscription order, the
notification fetch, fragmented reassembly, the app-name lookup, the
learned-address signal, the texts-never-mirrored rule, and the
wrong-device diagnosis all execute for real with only the radio layer
scripted. 190 unit tests and all four live harnesses pass.

To upgrade
Quit with the round power button, extract over the old folder, run
install.bat once, confirm v3.3.2, then Settings > Alerts > Choose
iPhone > Find my iPhone. Keep the phone unlocked and next to the PC
the first time; if Windows or the phone shows a pairing or allow
prompt, accept it. If Find reports that nothing proved to be your
iPhone, the fix is almost always on the phone: Settings > Bluetooth >
tap the (i) beside this PC > turn on Share System Notifications, then
Find again.
