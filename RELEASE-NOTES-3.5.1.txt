JRL Messages 3.5.1 - The skipped restart that left the link dead

What your 21:16 log caught
When you pressed Save, the link stopped to re-aim at the new address.
The old radio thread was mid-scan and slow to notice the stop, so the
scheduled restart waited four seconds, gave up ("Phone link thread
slow to stop; restart skipped this time"), and nothing ever tried
again. From that moment the link was dead until the next app start,
which is why your session went quiet after 21:16:24. Also visible in
the log: the address remembered at 21:14 had already rotated away by
21:16 ("was not found"), which is normal iPhone behavior the self-heal
probe handles, but only if the worker is actually running.

The fix
A restart that arrives while the old thread is still winding down is
no longer dropped. It is handed to that thread itself, which respawns
the link the moment it exits, and an explicit switch-off still cancels
everything cleanly. Three new tests pin the semantics, including the
race where the old thread exits in the very instant the handoff is
recorded.

Also: pressing "Phone Link pairing…" now writes a line to the
Activity panel, so your logs show when the QR flow was opened, which
this log could not tell me.

The one thing the log cannot show
Whether the Phone Link QR pairing itself completed, and whether a test
notification actually appeared inside Phone Link's own window. That
sighting is the proof that both the pairing and the phone's
notification permission exist; everything in this app is downstream of
it. The order that matters:

1. Phone Link pairing… > scan the QR with the iPhone camera > allow
   Link to Windows > finish its steps.
2. Send yourself an email and confirm the notification appears IN
   PHONE LINK.
3. Only then: Connect my iPhone, wait for the timed proof, Save.

If step 2 never shows a notification in Phone Link itself, the issue
lives entirely between Microsoft and Apple on your machine, and no
version of this app can succeed until it does; that is the honest
boundary.
