JRL Messages 3.4.2 - Your Activity log found all three remaining bugs

Thank you for that log. It was the most useful field report of this
whole effort: three distinct bugs, each visible in plain text.

Bug 1, the blocker: "Could not pair with device: FAILED", instantly
Windows aborted the pairing before anything reached your phone. Why:
pairing was being requested with only one "ceremony" (a simple
tap-to-confirm), but an iPhone negotiates a confirm-code ceremony,
where both screens show a number. When Windows is not told the app can
handle that ceremony, it gives up on the spot, promptless. 3.4.2 runs
the pairing through Windows' full ceremony machinery, requesting every
kind an iPhone can use. Practically: during Connect my iPhone you may
now see a code in the wizard ("Code 123456 - confirm it on the
iPhone"); the app accepts the Windows side for you, and you just tap
Pair on the phone. This was the wall every previous attempt hit.

Bug 2: "paired (LE) failed: Invalid parameter count", three times.
The Windows API bindings on your machine speak a slightly different
dialect than expected, so every paired-list query failed before it
began. The queries now try every known calling form and use the first
that binds, so the paired list can finally answer on your machine.

Bug 3: at 16:55 your log shows the BACKGROUND link attempting to pair
("Pairing with Apple device: confirm the prompt..."). That is the
invisible-prompt loop the ceremony was built to eliminate, and it was
still alive inside the background worker, firing prompts at your phone
at random moments where they expired unseen, and interfering with the
wizard. The background link now NEVER pairs. If it ever finds the bond
missing, it says exactly one thing ("Your iPhone wants a pairing
approval... use Connect my iPhone in Settings") and waits calmly.
