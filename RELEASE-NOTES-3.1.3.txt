JRL Messages 3.1.3 - Alerts while the app is open, list steering, hide zones

Alerts no longer assume you are watching
Until now, a text arriving into the conversation you had on screen in a
focused window was presented silently, on the theory that you were
looking at it. You are not always looking at it. From 3.1.3 every fresh
incoming text raises its popup and plays its sound unconditionally:
window closed to the tray, minimized, open beside your work, or sitting
on that exact conversation. Only two things stay quiet: notifications
set to Off, and messages Windows first saw more than 30 minutes ago
(old news never ambushes you late). Unread badges still respect what is
visibly on screen, so the thread you are actively reading does not
accumulate a stale count. The taskbar flash is added whenever the
window is not the active one.

The list steers to the new text
When a text arrives, the conversation list scrolls on its own so the
conversation that just moved to the top is actually visible, however
far down you had scrolled. It scrolls only: your selection and the
thread you are reading are never hijacked. Together with the popup, the
sound, and the unread badge, a new arrival is now impossible to miss
with the app in any state.

Hide zones: two actions per row
The accidental design you liked is now the intended design. Every
conversation row has two sides: the left opens it, the right edge hides
it. A conversation with unread messages advertises the hide action with
the full accent eye-off chip on hover. An already-read conversation
stays calm: hovering raises only a soft accent shade along its right
edge, and when your cursor is inside the zone itself the shade deepens
and a faint eye-off ghost appears with the pointer cursor, a subtle
"clicking here does something" exactly as requested. The click target
is the full-height right zone, comfortably larger than the old chip,
shared precisely between the painter and the mouse so what you see is
what you hit. Undo and the Hidden button behave as before.

Validation
- 117 automated tests pass: everything from 3.1.2 plus new regressions
  pinning the always-alert rule (no visibility gating on popups or
  sound, unread still visibility-aware), the list steering (both reload
  paths steer, scroll never selects or opens), and the hide-zone
  geometry and painting split (chip on unread, clipped low-alpha shade
  on read, zone-based click routing).
- The live storm harness additionally proves, under a real display: a
  popup and the alert signal fire with the conversation visibly open;
  the steering target is consumed; read-row zone hover renders; and all
  previous guarantees hold (flat window counts under churn, breaker
  engagement, warden neutralization, ledger drain, no event-loop
  stalls). Window, agent-channel, and pipeline harnesses pass
  unchanged.

Upgrade
1. Quit via the tray menu.
2. Extract this ZIP over the existing jrl-messages folder.
3. Run install.bat once.
4. Start JRL-Messages.bat and confirm v3.1.3 in the title bar.
