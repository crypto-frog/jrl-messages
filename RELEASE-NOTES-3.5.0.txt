JRL Messages 3.5.0 - Pairing handed to Phone Link; this app attaches

The truth about that QR dialog
You asked for pairing "like Phone Link does it", and you deserve the
straight answer about why theirs is smooth: the QR code is not the
pairing. It launches Link to Windows, a small Microsoft app that runs
ON THE IPHONE the moment you scan it, and that app performs the
phone-side pairing from inside iOS with Apple's blessing. That is why
the prompts are right, the (i) page is right, and the permissions
land. No Windows program can put software on your iPhone; only Apple's
App Store allows that. So their exact flow cannot be rebuilt, and I
will not pretend otherwise.

What CAN be done, and now is
Bluetooth pairings belong to Windows, not to the app that created
them. So 3.5.0 stops fighting on pairing entirely and divides the work
by strength:

- Phone Link's QR flow creates the pairing (Microsoft's app on the
  phone, correct iOS pages every time).
- This app attaches to that pairing and does everything else: finds
  the phone by proof, subscribes, proves the link with a live timed
  round trip, and presents every notification through YOUR popups,
  bell, tint, and sounds.

Concretely, in the picker:
- A new "Phone Link pairing…" button opens Microsoft's QR dialog (the
  exact one in your screenshot) with fallbacks if the app moved.
- "Connect my iPhone" is now attach-only. It NEVER pairs and NEVER
  removes pairings. If it finds your iPhone unpaired, it says exactly
  that and points to the Phone Link button; nothing is attempted
  behind your back and no prompts fire into the void.
- The app's own coached pairing ceremony still exists for machines
  without Phone Link, offered only as an advanced fallback.

The setup, start to finish
1. Choose iPhone… > "Phone Link pairing…" > scan the QR with the
   phone's camera > allow Link to Windows > finish its steps. Then
   prove it once: send yourself an email and confirm the notification
   shows in Phone Link. That single check proves the pairing AND the
   phone's notification permission end to end.
2. Press "Connect my iPhone". It attaches, tests, and shows the
   round-trip time. Press Save. Done permanently; reconnects,
   rotations, and restarts are handled.
3. Optional: turn off Phone Link's own notification banners inside
   Phone Link's settings so arrivals show only through this app; the
   pairing it created remains either way.

Validation
208 unit tests. New pins: default mode never pairs (an unpaired phone
yields the needs-Phone-Link outcome with zero pairing attempts), the
launcher has fallbacks, and the advanced ceremony still passes all its
prior tests behind the flag. The never-unpair pin from 3.4.4 stands.
All four live harnesses green.

To upgrade
Power button, extract, install.bat, confirm v3.5.0, then the three
setup steps above. Since you already have Phone Link's QR dialog open,
you can do step 1 right now, before even upgrading.
