JRL Messages 3.5.3
==================
(Reconstructed release. The original 3.5.3 build was lost; this note
and its code were rebuilt from the field record, and the fix is now
pinned by nine new tests so it cannot be lost again.)

What field report #13 said
"its says my phone is not paired in Link but it is." And the
screenshots proved it: Windows' own Bluetooth page showed TWO "iPhone"
entries, BOTH Connected, while this app announced "Your iPhone is not
paired with this PC yet."

What was actually wrong
Three things, stacked.

1. The per-address pairing question is BLIND. An iPhone reaches this
   PC through a rotating anonymous address. Asking Windows "is THIS
   address paired?" about a fresh rotation answers no, even while the
   bond sits in Windows under the phone's real identity. The app
   believed that answer and told you a lie.

2. Two bonds, one permission. An old pairing attempt and Phone Link's
   pairing can both survive as "iPhone" entries, and only one of them
   carries the phone's Share System Notifications permission. The app
   kept retrying whichever it hit first.

3. One refusal ended the wizard. The guided setup stopped at the first
   paired-pending or not-paired verdict, so a working second entry
   never got its turn.

The fix, in three parts

1. THE PAIRED LIST IS THE AUTHORITY. On a refused subscription, the
   worker and the wizard now consult the Windows paired LIST. Any
   iPhone entry there means this PC is paired, full stop; the verdict
   becomes the permission-switch coaching, and the "not paired with
   this PC" line is pinned out by test whenever the list holds an
   iPhone. The per-address record is only ever trusted when it says
   yes.

2. SIDELINE AND ROTATE. A failure sidelines the ACTUAL connected
   address. The next attempt goes straight to the other un-sidelined
   paired iPhone entry, connected directly by its identity (no wasted
   by-address scan). When every entry has been tried, the set resets
   to the most recent failure so retries alternate A, B, A, B. Retry
   pacing follows the evidence: 8 seconds while an un-tried sibling
   remains, the long wait only after both had their turn. A successful
   subscription clears the slate.

3. THE WIZARD KEEPS GOING. Refusal verdicts are recorded and the
   remaining candidates still get their turn. A working second entry
   beats an earlier refusal outright; paired-pending outranks
   needs-phone-link; and before the harshest verdict is ever returned,
   the paired list gets the last word and flips it to paired-pending.

One more honesty fix: a paired entry NAMED iPhone that answers without
the notification service is no longer called "probably not your
iPhone". iPhones publish that service intermittently, and only toward
connections they trust. The message now says the phone answered
without the service this time, and the app moves on to the sibling.

Nothing else moved. The agent, the message pipeline, and every
messaging feature are untouched; this release is entirely inside the
experimental Bluetooth stack and its wizard.
