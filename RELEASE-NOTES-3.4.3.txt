Where things actually stand
Your last two reports show the pairing is WORKING now: the prompt
reaches the phone, you approve it, and the phone shows Elmo as
connected. Two last problems remained, one mine and one Apple's.

Mine: when the phone was paired but still refused the notification
subscription, the app concluded the bond must be stale and DESTROYED
it, then paired again, endlessly. That loop is gone. A bond created in
the current ceremony is never torn down; the stale-bond reset runs at
most once and only for genuinely old trusts. When the app finds itself
paired but refused, it now treats that as a WIN: it saves your phone,
ends the ceremony with clear instructions, and the background link
quietly retries every minute so everything completes BY ITSELF the
moment the phone-side switch is flipped. No more trying and trying.

Apple's: because this pairing is a pure low-energy bond, your iPhone
does not recognize Elmo as a Windows PC the way the old classic
pairing did. It shows a plain placeholder, asks what KIND of device
Elmo is, and hides the (i) until you answer. So the final steps on the
phone are:

1. Settings > Bluetooth > Elmo. When it asks what kind of device this
   is, pick Other (it only affects audio routing, nothing else).
2. The (i) appears after that. Open it.
3. Turn ON Share System Notifications. If that switch is not listed
   yet, leave the app running near the phone for one minute (the
   link's retry is what makes iOS add the switch), then reopen the
   (i).
4. Done. Within a minute the bell should say "iPhone link connected".
   Then email yourself and lock the phone for the first mirrored
   notification.

All of this coaching now appears verbatim in the app itself: the
ceremony ends with it, and the background link's status says it too,
with your PC's actual name, instead of the old generic pairing advice.
