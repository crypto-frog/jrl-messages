JRL Messages 3.4.1 - The silent phone, explained and fixed

What "no prompt, nothing" told us
Your report was the missing clue. When the ceremony says it is waiting
for approval and the iPhone shows absolutely nothing, the cause is
almost always a STALE one-sided trust: Windows still holds an old
Bluetooth bond record for the phone (Phone Link created one long ago),
so when the app asks Windows to pair, Windows answers "already paired"
internally and sends nothing over the air. The phone has nothing to
show a prompt for, and the subscription keeps failing because the
phone no longer honors that old key. Both sides were technically
behaving correctly; the relationship was broken.

What 3.4.1 does about it
The Connect ceremony now detects exactly this state: connected fine,
subscription refused, and Windows claiming the device is paired. When
it sees that combination it announces "Found the problem", resets the
stale trust on the PC side, reconnects fresh, and only THEN triggers
the pairing, at which point your iPhone finally has a real pairing
request to show you. One honest side effect is stated out loud during
the reset: another app's old pairing to the phone (such as Phone Link)
may need redoing later, since the stale record it relied on is the
thing being cleared.

If the phone stays silent even after the reset, the remaining cause is
the mirror image (an old entry on the PHONE for this PC), and the
ceremony now tells you the exact fix by name: iPhone Settings >
Bluetooth > the (i) beside this PC's name > Forget This Device, then
run Connect my iPhone again. That coaching, with your PC's actual
name, appears right in the dialog and in the notes.

Small refinements from your screenshot
- The waiting messages now lead with "unlock the phone"; iOS hides
  pairing prompts behind the lock screen, so a locked phone looks
  exactly like a missing prompt.
- Every pairing outcome that Windows reports (completed, already
  paired, failed) is written into the visible notes, so nothing about
  this handshake is silent anymore.

Validation
200 unit tests. The new scripted-radio ceremony reproduces your exact
field case: a phone whose subscription is refused while Windows claims
it is paired must be bond-reset, freshly reconnected, and only then
paired, with the reset visible in the notes; a second test pins the
Forget This Device coaching. All four live harnesses green.

To upgrade and run
Quit with the power button, extract over the old folder, run
install.bat, confirm v3.4.1. Then Settings > Alerts > Choose iPhone… >
Connect my iPhone, with the phone UNLOCKED in your hand. Expect the
"Found the problem… resetting" message this time, then the pairing
prompt on the phone. Tap Pair, wait for "Connected, paired, and
proven", press Save, then email yourself and lock the phone to see the
first real mirrored notification.
