JRL Messages 3.5.2 - The cache that lied, and the loop that believed it

What your screenshots revealed
The paired list finally works, and it named the ghosts: "Jonathan's
A14" and "moto g54 5G" are Android phones (several mystery addresses
from earlier sessions were them, correctly rejected), and there is now
a paired entry named "iPhone", which strongly suggests your Phone Link
QR pairing completed. And yet the app kept saying that very entry
"does not serve iPhone notifications", every thirty seconds.

Three bugs made that lie stick, and all three are fixed.

1. The Windows service cache. Windows remembers a paired device's
   service list, and the app was reading that memory instead of asking
   the phone. One stale snapshot, taken at a bad moment during the
   earlier pairing chaos, makes a genuine iPhone look service-less on
   every later connect, forever. Every service check now forces live
   discovery and never trusts the cache: the worker's sessions, the
   proof probe, and the wizard, all three.

2. The poisoned shortcut loop. When the app decided to re-identify
   your phone by proof, its search still tried the stored paired entry
   first, connected to it successfully, hit the same cached lie, and
   never reached the proof step. Your 21:30-21:32 log is that loop.
   Once proof is pending, the search now skips the stored-address and
   paired shortcuts entirely and goes straight to testing the live
   anonymous broadcasts.

3. A silent two-release-old bug the same investigation exposed: the
   background link's paired-list lookup has been failing quietly since
   3.3.2 because of a data-shape mismatch, which is why the worker
   never benefited from the paired list at all. Fixed and pinned so
   the shapes can never drift apart again.

What to expect after upgrading
Power button, extract, install.bat, confirm v3.5.2. Then just watch
the bell for a minute or two: with your "iPhone" pairing already in
place, the first live-discovery connect should either subscribe (bell
says "iPhone link connected", and the email-and-lock test should
mirror) or, if the phone still withholds permission, you will get the
precise paired-but-waiting message about the Share System
Notifications switch rather than the false "not your iPhone" verdict.
The Test link button gives the same answer on demand.

And the standing question that decides the last mile: does a test
notification appear inside Phone Link's own window? If Phone Link
shows it and this app connects but stays quiet, say exactly that; if
Phone Link itself shows nothing, the phone-side permission from the QR
flow did not finish, and rerunning Phone Link's setup is the fix.

Validation
213 unit tests (new: the cache-bypass is asserted on the live session
fakes; the proof-escalation skip is exercised end to end with the
stored address proven untouched; the paired-row shape is pinned) and
all four live harnesses green.
