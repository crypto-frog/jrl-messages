JRL Messages 3.4.4 - No more automatic unpairing, and the clean-slate
recipe that gets you out of the ghost-entry trap

What your report proved
Elmo showing on the phone with no (i), even after answering the
device-kind question, means the phone's entry is a ghost: a leftover
shell from the earlier pair-and-destroy loop, holding a half-bond that
matches nothing on Windows. iOS gives such remnants no detail page,
which locks you out of the notifications switch entirely. The
automatic "stale bond reset" I shipped in 3.4.1 is what created this
wreckage, and I own that. It is now removed permanently: the app will
NEVER unpair anything again. When it finds itself paired but refused,
it stops, saves the win, and coaches; when a trust is genuinely
broken, the repair is done BY HAND with both sides visible, using the
recipe below.

THE CLEAN-SLATE RECIPE (do this once; it needs no app upgrade)
1. On Windows: Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Devices > Remove any
   iPhone or unknown entries.
2. On the iPhone: tap Forget This Device on any 'elmo' entry that
   offers an (i). If the entry shows no (i), toggle Bluetooth off and
   on; if the ghost lingers, restart the phone once.
3. Pair the OFFICIAL way: Windows Settings > Add device > Bluetooth >
   pick your iPhone (unlocked, on its Bluetooth screen), confirm the
   code on both. The phone now lists elmo as a real computer WITH an
   (i) page, exactly like you remember from before.
4. Open the app and run Settings > Alerts > Choose iPhone… > Connect
   my iPhone once, so the notification channel attaches to that
   healthy trust. Approve anything either screen shows.
5. On the phone: elmo > (i) > turn ON Share System Notifications. If
   the switch is not listed yet, wait one minute (the app's retry is
   what makes iOS add it) and reopen the (i).
6. Watch the bell for "iPhone link connected", then email yourself
   and lock the phone.

What changed in the app
- The ceremony never unpairs, ever (pinned by a test that fails the
  build if unpairing ever returns). Already-paired plus refused now
  ends immediately as "paired, waiting on the phone's switch" with
  the address saved and the background link finishing the job on its
  own.
- Every coaching text (ceremony, background link, setup guide) now
  carries the clean-slate recipe with your PC's actual name, including
  the no-(i) ghost case, and makes Windows-Settings pairing the
  official first step.

Validation
206 unit tests and all four live harnesses green, including the new
pin: an already-paired phone with a refused subscription produces the
paired-pending result with zero pairing attempts and zero unpairs.

Upgrade order
Do the recipe above first (steps 1 to 3 need no app at all), then
upgrade (power button, extract, install.bat, confirm v3.4.4), then
step 4 onward.
