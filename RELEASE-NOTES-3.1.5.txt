JRL Messages 3.1.5 - The silent alerts, found: texts to yourself now ring

What was actually wrong
Your screenshot solved it. The test message was "Zzzz" sent to your own
number: the top conversation showed "You: Zzzzz". A text to yourself is
the natural way to test the line, but Apple marks it as SENT BY YOU on
every device it lands on. The app, like every prior version, treated
anything sent-by-you as an outgoing echo and deliberately never created
an alert for it. So the message arrived and displayed (which you saw),
while the alert ledger recorded nothing (which you heard). The Test
alert button worked because it exercises the popup and sound machinery
directly without going through that ledger. Nothing in 3.1.4's pipeline
was broken; the pipeline was never asked to run. Alerts from OTHER
people were ringing the whole time.

What 3.1.5 does
1. Self-conversations alert. A text in a one-to-one conversation with
   one of your own numbers or emails now creates the same durable alert
   record as any other arrival: popup, sound, unread badge, list steer,
   all of it, even though Apple stamps it sent-by-you.
2. Your identities are learned and taught. The account your Mac reports
   is recognized automatically the moment the agent connects, and it is
   remembered so alerts work from the first second after a restart.
   Settings gained a Self-texts row: a checkbox to turn the behavior
   off, and a field for your OTHER own numbers or emails, comma
   separated. If you text yourself at your phone number, put that
   number there (for example +15875550123); the Mac usually reports
   only your Apple ID email, so the phone-number self-conversation
   needs this one entry to be recognized.
3. Texts sent from THIS app never alert, anywhere. The moment a send
   completes, its message row is stamped so no later rescan, restart,
   or reconciliation pass can ever turn your own PC-sent text into a
   popup. Replying to yourself from the PC stays quiet; the phone-typed
   side still announces itself.
4. Ordinary conversations are untouched. A from-me text in any normal
   conversation still never alerts; groups are never treated as
   self-conversations.

Proof, not hope
- Seven new regression tests pin the whole behavior: self-chat texts
  create exactly one alert record even across rescans, app-sent texts
  never do even when re-read later, other chats and groups keep the old
  exclusion, the switch restores the old behavior, identities survive a
  restart, and the settings and agent wiring is verified.
- The live pipeline harness (real agent against a scripted Mac) now
  boots, learns the account from the server, receives a phone-typed
  self-text, and proves the durable alert record appears; then sends a
  self-text through the real outbox and proves silence holds through
  reconciler rescans.
- The live window harness raises a real card and sound from a
  sent-by-you self-text, and proves a from-me text to a friend stays
  silent.
- Full suite: 133 unit tests plus all four live harnesses pass.

To upgrade
Quit with the round power button, extract the zip over the old folder,
run install.bat once, and confirm the Settings dialog shows v3.1.5.
Then open Settings and add your phone number to the Self-texts field,
and send yourself a Zzzz.
