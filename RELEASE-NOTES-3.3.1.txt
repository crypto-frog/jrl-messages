JRL Messages 3.3.1 - Your iPhone now appears in the picker

The bug you found
You scanned and saw your Bose speaker but never your iPhone. That was
my bug, built on top of an Apple behavior: iPhones deliberately
broadcast Bluetooth with no name and a rotating anonymous address so
strangers cannot identify or track them. Your phone WAS in the scan
results the whole time; the picker threw away nameless devices, which
threw away every iPhone on earth. Speakers happily shout their names,
which is why they showed.

What 3.3.1 does
1. The picker now reads the Windows PAIRED device list first, where
   your iPhone lives under its real name with its stable address.
   Since Phone Link already paired your phone to Windows, it will
   simply be there, listed first and tagged "paired with this PC".
   No live scan is even needed to find it.
2. The live scan no longer discards anonymous devices: anything
   broadcasting Apple manufacturer data is listed and labeled
   "Apple device (name hidden while broadcasting)".
3. The background link resolves your phone the same way: paired
   identity first (which also heals a re-pairing that changed the
   address, by matching the remembered name), then the address, then a
   scan that recognizes anonymous Apple broadcasters, and it only
   auto-picks an anonymous one when exactly one is in range so it can
   never latch onto your AirPods.
4. The probe tool (tools\phone_link_probe.py) got the same treatment,
   so its listing now matches what the app sees.

Two smaller bugs the investigation shook out
- For the first ten minutes after a PC reboot, a connection-loss entry
  would silently skip the bell (a rate limiter mistook boot-time zero
  for a timestamp). Found because it made my own test harness fail
  once per fresh container; fixed and pinned with tests.
- A link flapping at the edge of Bluetooth range could fill the bell
  with "restored" entries while suppressing the losses. Transitions
  now record in strict pairs: first connect, one loss, one recovery,
  then quiet until things are genuinely stable again.

About Phone Link
You can leave Phone Link installed or remove it; this app no longer
depends on it either way. Its one lasting contribution is the pairing
it already created, which is exactly what the new picker uses. If you
do remove it, the pairing stays (it belongs to Windows, not to Phone
Link), so nothing needs redoing.

Validation
184 unit tests (9 new: address formatting, paired-first ordering and
dedupe, Apple-broadcast labeling, boot-clock transitions, flap
symmetry) and all four live harnesses green, three consecutive storm
runs. The radio handshake itself still proves out on your machine, so
the honest next step is yours: upgrade, open the picker, and your
iPhone should be the first row.

To upgrade
Quit with the round power button, extract over the old folder, run
install.bat once, confirm v3.3.1, then Settings > Alerts > Choose
iPhone.
