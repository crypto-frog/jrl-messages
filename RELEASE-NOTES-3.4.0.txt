JRL Messages 3.4.0 - Connect my iPhone: one guided step, and the real
missing piece found

What your screenshot finally revealed
Three things at once, and together they told the whole story.

First, seven anonymous Apple devices all "very close": a single iPhone
broadcasts several separate anonymous identities at the same time, so
choosing from that list by hand was never a fair task, and I should
not have asked you to do it.

Second, and this is the important one: everything you described (the
paired list empty, Find verifying the phone but the link never
holding, "not connected" forever) fits one root cause. Your phone and
your PC almost certainly have no Bluetooth LE bond. Phone Link's
pairing does not create the specific trust that notifications travel
over. Without it, every connection attempt ends with the iPhone asking
for a pairing approval, and that prompt was appearing (and expiring)
on your phone at random moments inside a background retry loop, while
nobody was looking at it. The setup was failing at a step neither of
us could see.

Third, two honest UI bugs you caught: selecting a row in the list
showed no highlight at all (a stylesheet gap; the selection was
happening invisibly), and the diagnostics that would have explained
the empty paired list were hidden in a debug log.

The fix: Connect my iPhone
The picker now has one primary button that does the entire setup as a
single supervised ceremony while you watch the phone:

1. It finds the phone by proof (the only nearby device that can serve
   iPhone notifications to this PC), never by name or address.
2. It connects and, the moment pairing is needed, tells you in large
   amber text: ACTION NEEDED, look at your iPhone right now and tap
   Pair / Allow. It then waits patiently, about 40 seconds across five
   retries, while you approve; no more prompts firing into the void.
3. It subscribes, then proves the link by asking the phone a live
   question and showing the round-trip time.
4. It saves the result itself. You press Save in Settings and you are
   done, permanently: the approval creates the durable Bluetooth trust,
   so every future reconnect is automatic, the paired list starts
   knowing your phone, and address rotation stops mattering.

The candidates it examines are tried strongest-signal first (hold the
phone next to the PC), a device without the notification service is
skipped without ever bothering it with a pairing prompt, and at most
three candidates are ever asked to pair, so a neighbor's device cannot
be pestered. Every skipped device and the reason is reported in the
dialog and in the Activity panel.

Also fixed and improved
- List selections are visible everywhere pickers are used (highlight
  in your accent color; double-click also chooses).
- The background link now pauses while the setup wizard owns the
  radio, and resumes whether you Save or Cancel.
- A restart of the link a moment after stopping it can no longer be
  silently swallowed.
- Setup coaching, closeness hints, and every failure reason are shown
  in the dialog itself, word-wrapped, as they happen.

How to run it
Upgrade (quit with the power button, extract, install.bat, confirm
v3.4.0), then: Settings > Alerts > Choose iPhone… > Connect my iPhone,
with the phone UNLOCKED in your hand. When the amber text appears, tap
Pair on the phone (and allow the Windows "Add a device" message if one
shows). Wait for "Connected, paired, and proven", press Save, then
prove it end to end: send yourself an email and lock the phone.

Validation
198 unit tests (two new scripted-radio ceremonies: the full
pair-and-prove flow, and an unapproved pairing failing with honest
notes; the AirPods-like decoy is skipped without ever being asked to
pair) and all four live harnesses green. The pairing approval itself
is the one human step no test can perform; that is exactly why it is
now coached out loud instead of hidden.
