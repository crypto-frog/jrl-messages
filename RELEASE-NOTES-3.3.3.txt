JRL Messages 3.3.3 - Test link button, live status, stubborn reconnects

What your bell showed
Find my iPhone worked and verified your phone, but the link dropped 25
minutes later ("not reachable"), never got back, and after that one
entry the app went quiet. You had no way to see whether it was even
still trying, and no way to test it on demand. Both are fixed, and the
reconnect logic learned from the exact failure in your screenshot.

The Test link button
Settings > Alerts now has Test link, right beside Choose iPhone. It is
a real test, not a decoration: it asks your iPhone a live question
over the Bluetooth link (the display name of its own Settings app) and
pops the answer with the round-trip time, through your normal alert
style and sound. A pass proves the connection, the encryption, and
both notification channels end to end. When things are not working,
the button says exactly where they stopped, in plain words:

- mirroring off or no phone chosen -> it says to set that up first
- link not running -> it starts it and says to test again shortly
- not connected right now -> it forces an immediate reconnect attempt
  (no waiting out the retry timer) and tells you to watch the bell
- connected -> the live round trip runs and the timing pops

One honest caveat is included in the success message itself: the one
thing this PC cannot measure is the phone-side "Share System
Notifications" switch, so if the test passes but real notifications
stay quiet, that switch is the culprit (iPhone Settings > Bluetooth >
the (i) next to this PC).

You can always see the link's state now
The same Settings row shows it live: "Connected now" or "Not connected
right now; the app keeps retrying". And during a long outage the bell
posts a quiet "iPhone still unreachable" reminder every half hour, so
silence in the bell now always means connected and quiet, never
gave-up.

Smarter about the failure you actually hit
iPhones rotate their anonymous Bluetooth address regularly. Your link
was likely stuck retrying an address that had gone stale. Now, after
two failed connection attempts in a row, the app stops trusting the
remembered address and re-identifies the phone by proof (the same
notification-service probe the Find button uses), then remembers the
new address. The dead-address loop cannot persist.

Also tidied
The Settings row no longer shows the long "verified..." sentence as
the phone's name; it stores "Your iPhone".

Reminder: how to see a real notification end to end
Email yourself and lock the phone, or build a one-tap tester once on
the iPhone: Shortcuts app > + > Show Notification, add it to the home
screen. Texting yourself does not test the Bluetooth mirror, by
design; texts already alert through the Mac and are never doubled.

Validation
196 unit tests (6 new: the live round trip through the scripted radio,
button routing in every state, the half-hourly outage reminder, wiring
pins) and all four live harnesses green.

To upgrade
Quit with the round power button, extract over the old folder, run
install.bat, confirm v3.3.3, then Settings > Alerts > Test link.
